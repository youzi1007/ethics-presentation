What about bias?

A selection process may disproportionately disadvantage protected groups even when it does not explicitly use protected traits. Demonstrating that requires real data and careful evaluation. This prototype instead focuses on a smaller question you can test directly: how changing apparently neutral weights changes who gets the opportunity to be seen.

Methodology and reading: Fictional candidates and deterministic weighted scores; no backend or AI. Hiring technologies can raise disability discrimination issues, as described by the U.S. Department of Justice guidance on AI and disability discrimination in hiring. See also the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. This is not legal advice or a validated assessment tool.
