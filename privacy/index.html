        About the experiment. A fictional educational demonstration. The simulation does not set tracking cookies, send your choices to an analytics or advertising provider, use device location, or create real behavioral profiles. This preview currently loads real product photography from Unsplash, so your browser makes normal image requests to Unsplash servers; the photo sources are listed below. Our simulated first-party records, optional sharing rules, and product outcomes are illustrative, not descriptions of any particular company. For real-world background, see the FTC's explanation of online tracking and New York's consumer guide.
